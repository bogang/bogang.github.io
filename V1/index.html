Factory strength

A direct manufacturing partner for long-term film sourcing.

Zhejiang Haibin Film Technology Co., Ltd. is located in Binhai Zone, Shaoxing, Zhejiang. The plant is close to Hangzhou airport and supports shipments through Ningbo Port, helping overseas buyers coordinate samples, production and logistics with fewer handoffs.

Advanced production equipment

Two high-configuration Bruckner BOPP lines, plus British Atlas and German Kampf slitting equipment, support stable roll quality and precise finishing.

Specialized thin film capability

Haibin focuses on 3-8 micron BOPP capacitor film with annual capacity of more than 4,600 tons for high-tech industrial applications.

Source-to-shipment control

Raw material selection, casting, stretching, corona treatment, winding, slitting, packing and shipment checks are managed through a documented process.

Group manufacturing background

Haibin belongs to Zhejiang Wuming Group, with experience in PET and POF film production and established industrial film know-how.

Quality confidence
