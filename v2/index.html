Frequently Asked

What buyers ask first.

What is your MOQ for capacitor-grade BOPP film?
From 1 ton for new trials. Most established capacitor makers order 5–20 tons per shipment. We support small-quantity validation runs before you commit to a full production order.
What is the typical lead time from order to delivery?
15 working days for free sample rolls. 25–35 working days for production orders after PO confirmation, FOB Ningbo.
Do you supply roughened film (RP / RR) for oil-impregnated capacitors?
Yes — single-side roughened (RP) and double-side roughened (RRP) grades are available, designed for compatibility with PXE, DPE, M/DBT, and MIDEL 7131 impregnation oils.
What payment terms and Incoterms do you accept?
T/T 30% deposit, 70% against copy of B/L. FOB Ningbo / CIF / EXW available. L/C accepted for established accounts.
